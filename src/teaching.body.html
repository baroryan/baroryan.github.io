Field teaching · Peru, 2019

Field Teaching in Peru

During my Ph.D. at Columbia University's Lamont–Doherty Earth Observatory, I designed, organized, and secured funding for a 12-day graduate field course across Peru for twelve students. Traversing the Andes from the forearc to the Altiplano, the course explored mountain building, active faulting, volcanic arcs, tropical glaciers, and the geomorphic and climatic signatures of El Niño.

The field guide, field notes, and itinerary are available here for anyone planning a similar course or looking for teaching resources on the geology and tectonics of the central Andes.
